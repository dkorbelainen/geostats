# Rating forecast benchmark

Generated by `benchmarks/forecast/report.py` at `daaa453`. Do not edit.

## Setup

- Data: 384215 snapshots of 7513 accounts, 2026-07-01 to 2026-10-12 (exclusive).
- A forecast is made every 7 days from 2026-07-15, 7 and 30 days ahead, for accounts with enough history and a rating known at the target date.
- Models are selected on forecasts resolved by 2026-09-09 (5-fold cross-validation grouped by account) and tested on forecasts made from that date.
- Intervals: cluster bootstrap over accounts, 2000 resamples.
- Direction: share of correct signs where the predicted and the actual change are both non-zero.

## Criteria

1–3 were fixed after one exploratory look at the test period with three configurations; 4 is blind.

1. 7 days: MAE below no change, interval excludes zero: **pass**
2. 30 days: MAE below no change, interval excludes zero: **pass**
3. No rating tier is worse than no change at either horizon: **pass**
4. 7 days on forecasts made after 2026-10-12: **pending**, needs a later export
